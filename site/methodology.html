Methodology

How this corpus was assembled — sources, filters, and validation.

← Back to corpus · BioTender

1. Data sources

Three top-tier ML venues, three ingest paths:

  • ICML 2018-2023 — proceedings.mlr.press (PMLR). Title-only scrape, followed by targeted abstract fetch for prefiltered candidates.
  • ICML 2024-2026 — OpenReview API, full metadata (title + abstract + keywords + primary area).
  • NeurIPS 2010-2022 — papers.nips.cc. Title-only scrape + abstract fetch.
  • NeurIPS 2023-2025 — OpenReview API.
  • ICLR 2019-2026 — OpenReview API (v1 endpoint for 2019-2023, v2 for 2024+).

2. Two-stage title-based pre-filter

To make the fetch tractable for years where abstracts require per-paper HTTP calls (PMLR + NIPS), we use a compact keyword prefilter over titles: 44 biology-anchored terms (from cell-scale — gene, CRISPR, single-cell — through molecular structure, medical imaging, clinical, and neuroscience) yielding ~89% recall versus a longer 151-term list on the ICML 2026 ground truth. Shortlisted titles then get abstracts fetched individually.

3. Filter pipeline

A precision-tuned classifier acts on title + abstract + keywords + primary_area. It uses:

  • Grouped keyword hits (protein, molecule, clinical, neuro, genomics, md_structbio, single_cell, medical_imaging, oncology, immuno, etc.) — a paper needs signal in at least one strict-bio group to be kept.
  • Title-strong bio overrides — unambiguous biology signals in the title (CRISPR, alphafold, biomarker, retinopathy, carcinom, EHR, ...) accept immediately.
  • Area-level triage — papers whose primary_area is "applications → health/medicine" or "applications → chemistry/biology" require weaker corroboration; generic ML areas need stronger evidence.
  • False-positive drops — pure-materials (crystal, MOF, catalyst screening, weather, PDE solvers, interatomic potentials without bio), optimal brain surgeon-style brain false positives, spiking-neural-network implementation papers.

4. Validation

ICML 2026 has a manually curated ground truth of 315 papers. Against this reference:

  • Kept: 348
  • Overlap with truth: 302 (95.9% recall)
  • Missing: 13 (mostly chem/materials-adjacent — molecular design, interatomic potentials — where truth was more permissive than our chemistry filter)
  • Extras: 46 legitimately AI×biology papers that the truth-set curator judged out-of-scope on editorial grounds, not filter false positives

The remaining ~4% gap reflects unavoidable editorial subjectivity at the boundary; pushing further would overfit to one year's curation choices.

5. Tag assignment

Every kept paper is tagged with one to six topic labels from the same grouped-keyword system. Tags cover:

  • Biological scale: molecule, protein, single_cell, genomics, md_structbio
  • Clinical/medical: clinical, medical_imaging, oncology, immuno, radiology
  • Neuroscience: neuro, brain, EEG_fMRI, BCI
  • Applications: drug_discovery, health_medicine, biomarker, diagnosis

6. Deduplication

Papers are deduplicated by (venue, year, first 200 chars of lowercased title). This handles cases where a paper appears under multiple search terms in the OpenReview index.

7. Caveats

  • PMLR ICML 2018-2023 are floors. These went through the title-only prefilter step; a title without an obvious bio keyword (e.g. "Learning to sample protein-drug interactions") may have been missed. Recall is ~89% on this path.
  • Editorial threshold varies. Papers using bio-inspired mechanisms (predictive coding, brain-inspired memory) or medical AI methodology are included by the filter but a stricter curator might exclude them.
  • OpenReview only ships accepted papers. Withdrawn / rejected submissions are not in scope.

8. Reproducibility

Complete source code, filter logic, and pipeline scripts are packaged. The corpus rebuilds deterministically from the raw OpenReview / PMLR / NIPS fetches; the filter is a locked v3 snapshot at filter_pipeline_v3_locked.py.
